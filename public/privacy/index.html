Privacy Policy

Your learning should not require your financial identity.

RootWise is built to help people understand money without asking them to surrender unnecessary personal or financial information.

Effective date: August 4, 2026

Our plain-language promise: RootWise is an educational platform. We do not ask learners to enter bank account numbers, card numbers, Social Security numbers, passwords, verification codes, or other sensitive financial credentials into lessons, workbooks, assessments, or Ask Sage.

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RootWise handles information when you visit the RootWise website, use the RootWise learning experience, complete assessments, save lesson progress, write in workbook or reflection fields, use Ask Sage, or request narration.

This policy applies to the RootWise website and web application. A future school edition, Kids Corner, Business Hub, paid account, mobile application, or other service may include additional privacy notices before it collects information not described here.

2. Information RootWise currently handles

Information stored locally on your device

The current RootWise experience may store the following information in your browser’s local storage:

  • An optional first name or nickname;
  • Assessment answers and learning preferences;
  • Lesson progress and completion status;
  • Workbook entries, reflections, and practice responses; and
  • Other settings needed to continue your learning experience.

This information remains in the browser and device you used unless a particular feature clearly tells you otherwise. Clearing browser data, using private browsing, changing devices, or reinstalling a browser may erase locally stored information.

Information you intentionally submit to connected features

When you choose to use Ask Sage, the text you submit is sent to RootWise server functions and service providers used to generate an educational response. When you request connected narration, the lesson text needed to create or deliver audio may also be processed by a server function or voice service.

Private workbook and reflection entries are not automatically sent to Ask Sage. They are only transmitted if you intentionally copy, type, or submit that content into a connected feature.

Basic technical information

Like most websites, RootWise hosting and security providers may automatically process limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, requested pages, timestamps, error logs, and network or security events. This information is used to deliver the site, diagnose failures, protect the service, and prevent abuse.

3. Information you should not provide

Do not enter or upload sensitive information into RootWise, including:

  • Bank, credit union, brokerage, loan, or payment account numbers;
  • Credit or debit card numbers;
  • Social Security numbers, tax identification numbers, or government identification numbers;
  • Passwords, PINs, security answers, authentication codes, or recovery codes;
  • Exact credit reports, tax returns, bank statements, medical records, or legal case files;
  • Confidential information belonging to an employer, customer, student, child, or another person; or
  • Any information you do not have permission to share.

Use fictional, rounded, category-level, approximate, or redacted examples whenever personal context is useful for learning.

4. How information is used

RootWise uses information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide lessons, assessments, tools, narration, and Ask Sage responses;
  • Remember progress and preferences on your device;
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service;
  • Understand whether pages and features function as intended;
  • Comply with legal obligations; and
  • Protect RootWise, its users, and the public from misuse or harm.

RootWise does not use workbook entries to make lending, credit, employment, insurance, housing, or other eligibility decisions.

5. Sale, advertising, and sharing

RootWise does not sell your personal information. RootWise does not use sensitive financial information for targeted advertising.

Information may be processed by vendors that help operate the service, such as hosting, security, artificial intelligence, voice, analytics, and error-monitoring providers. Those providers receive only the information reasonably needed to perform their services and are subject to their own contractual and legal obligations.

RootWise may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to lawful process, to investigate fraud or security threats, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or transfer of business assets. Any successor would remain responsible for information under applicable law and the privacy commitments in effect at the time of transfer.

6. Cookies, local storage, and analytics

RootWise uses browser storage to preserve learning progress and preferences. Hosting and service providers may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for security, performance, fraud prevention, session management, or basic measurement.

If RootWise later introduces nonessential analytics, advertising technology, or additional tracking, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated before that technology is used.

7. Children and student privacy

The current public RootWise adult learning experience is not designed to collect personal information from children under 13. Children should not submit personal information through the adult experience or Ask Sage.

Kids Corner and any school-directed version of RootWise will use separate age-appropriate safeguards, restricted features, and additional notices before collecting information from children or students. RootWise will not knowingly launch child-directed data collection without the consent and controls required by applicable law.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the current service, contact RootWise so the information can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

8. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored learning information remains on your device until it is removed by the application, you clear site or browser data, or the browser removes it.

Information processed by connected server features, logs, or service providers is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the feature, maintain security, resolve errors, comply with law, or establish and defend legal rights. Retention periods may differ by provider and type of record.

Until RootWise offers online accounts, there may be no reliable way to identify or retrieve locally stored information from RootWise systems because that information may exist only on your device.

9. Security

RootWise uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the current service. No website, browser storage system, internet transmission, or third-party platform can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and browser. Avoid using shared or public devices for private reflections, and remove local site data when you no longer want it stored on a device.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

You may choose not to provide an optional name, not to use Ask Sage or narration, and not to enter private details into workbook fields. You can remove locally stored RootWise information by clearing site data for RootWise in your browser settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about certain personal data RootWise holds. Some rights may not apply when RootWise cannot reasonably verify your identity or when the information exists only in your local browser.

RootWise will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right.

11. External links

Lessons may link to government agencies, educational sources, or other third-party websites. RootWise does not control those sites and this policy does not govern their privacy or security practices. Review the privacy terms of any external service before providing information.

12. International use

RootWise is operated from the United States. If you use RootWise from another country, information processed by connected features may be transferred to and handled in the United States or other locations where RootWise service providers operate, subject to applicable law.

13. Changes to this policy

RootWise may update this policy as the product, law, or service providers change. Material changes will be identified by a new effective date and, when appropriate, a notice within the application before the changes take effect.

14. Contact

Privacy questions, requests, and reports may be submitted through the RootWise Contact page once the public support channel is connected and tested. Until then, do not send sensitive information through an unverified address or social-media message.

For a privacy request, describe the feature involved and the type of information at issue. Never include passwords, full account numbers, Social Security numbers, or authentication codes.
